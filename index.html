The idea behind the work

Meet the researcher

Salam, I'm Syrine, a PhD candidate at the SMART Lab of the Institut Supérieur de Gestion de Tunis. I build deep learning and optimization models for environmental, social and governance data, so that sustainable investment decisions rest on signals people can trust.

My work forecasts ESG volatility, reconciles the disagreement between rating agencies, and predicts governance controversies before they surface. Alongside the PhD, I teach computer science: algorithms, programming, databases and web development. The classroom keeps my research honest, and the research keeps my teaching curious.
